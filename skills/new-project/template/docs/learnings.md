# Learnings

Append-only log of lessons: bugs and their root causes, dead ends, surprises, and things that worked better than expected. Entries are numbered L-001 and up and are never deleted. This is the record of what the project taught; `/retro` turns the best entries into reusable lessons.

Template for a new entry:

```markdown
## L-nnn: Title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Task:** T-nnn
- **What happened:** the symptom, error or surprise
- **Root cause:** why it really happened
- **Fix or outcome:** what we did
- **Lesson:** the general rule, usable in other projects
```
